BEFORE YOU START: TWO-STEP VERIFICATION

Both logins are protected by two-step verification. Our team approves each sign-in through an authenticator app. Please let us know the day and time you plan to test, so we can be ready to approve your sign-in straight away.

FLOW 1: Member books → Staff approves → Member is notified
Follow these steps exactly, in this order.

Member portal
Sign in → Book → Equipment → 1 · Date: a day at least 2 days ahead → 2 · Your hours: tap a free window → drag Start / End → tap a stove and a grill → Review request → Send request
→ My bookings → the request shows Waiting (within about 1 minute)

Staff app
Sign in → Bookings → Requests → click the request → type a note, e.g. "See you Monday" → Approve

Member portal
Refresh the page → bell shows Booking confirmed (within about 1 minute) → My bookings shows Approved with your note → Schedule → the booking is on the calendar

FLOW 2: Double-booking check → Decline → Cancel → Staff books for a member
Do this after Flow 1. Follow these steps exactly, in this order.

A. Double-booking is blocked (member portal)
Book → Equipment → same day and overlapping hours as your approved booking → the same stove shows Taken till … and cannot be selected → other equipment is still Free

B. Staff decline a request
Member portal: Book → a different day → any equipment → Review request → Send request
→ Staff app: Bookings → Requests → click it → type a reason → Decline
→ Member portal: refresh → My bookings shows Declined with the reason

C. Member cancels (member portal)
My bookings → open the approved booking from Flow 1 → Cancel booking → confirm → status shows Cancelled (within about 1 minute) and the hours are returned
Within 24 hours of the start, the button reads Ask to cancel and staff decide instead.

D. Staff book on behalf of a member (staff app)
Bookings → Book → Member: Test Kitchen Co. → What: Kitchen → date, from and to → select equipment (Ctrl / Cmd-click for several) → Book
→ Member portal: refresh → My bookings shows it as Approved

E. Staff schedule (staff app)
Bookings → Schedule → Calendar / Day by equipment / 7-day overview → Today and the arrows move between days → Refresh loads the latest

FLOW 3: Staff publish a notice → Member reads it → Staff withdraw it
Follow these steps exactly, in this order.

Staff app
Notices → Title → Notice text → Category (e.g. Maintenance) → Pin to top: On → Hide after: leave empty → Publish → the notice appears under Published

Member portal
Refresh the page → bell shows the notice → a banner shows it on other pages → Notices → pinned notice is first → try the category tabs → Mark all as read

Staff app
Notices → Withdraw next to the notice → it moves to Drafts and withdrawn

Member portal
Refresh the page → the notice is gone

Optional: Save as draft keeps a notice private to staff until you publish it.
