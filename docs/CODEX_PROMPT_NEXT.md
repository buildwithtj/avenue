## Originality rule (added after the client-risk note): do NOT copy the old system
The client's old Nexudus portal (see `docs/evidence/2026-11-16-nexudus-*.jpg`) must not be recognisable in our booking flow or look, or the client may object. The old flow was: choose a "room" (Cook Station) > single/multiple-day month calendar with Today/Tomorrow and 1/2/3-hour chips and start/end drop-downs > a long list of equipment cards each with an "Add to booking" button and "+$0.00" > a right-hand "Summary" panel with a green "Continue" button, a yellow "A member of our team will contact you" note and a red "The booking you requested cannot be placed" box. Our current four-step wizard (mode > when > equipment list > review) still follows that same shape, so REPLACE it completely:
- **Book on the board.** The primary booking experience is the schedule itself: equipment rows by time columns (day view, plus a week strip). The member drags (or taps start then end) on the free space of the equipment they need to create their booking block; blocks that clash are impossible to draw (they snap or show why). One board, no wizard, no "Add to booking" buttons.
- **Start from what you are making, not from a list.** Offer "kits" (Hot line, Bakery and ovens, Prep only, Whole kitchen, Dining room, Private event) that pre-select the right equipment and prep tables, then let the member tweak by tapping equipment chips on the board. Equipment is shown as a compact, grouped toggle row with availability dots, not as stacked cards with buttons.
- **Smart time first.** A "Find my time" sheet: member picks kit + length, the app proposes the best 3 start times (earliest free, least disruptive, soonest) using `nextFreeSlots`; one tap books the draft.
- **Confirm in a bottom sheet, not a side panel.** A slim sheet shows what/when/equipment, early entry and cleaning times and hours used; the primary button says "Request this slot" (not "Continue"); the approval note, no-payment note and cancellation note are phrased in our own words in `src/avenue/copy.ts`.
- **Errors in our voice.** Replace any "cannot be placed" style message with a helpful suggestion ("Stove #1 is busy until 5 PM. Try 5:30 PM, or use Stove #2").
- **Different look.** Do not reuse the old olive-green button + white-card layout. Keep our brand tokens but use a distinct visual language: dark ink board canvas, bold time ruler, equipment pills, smooth drag with spring settle, strong typographic hierarchy; no copied copy, icons, layout order or colour scheme from the old screens. Do not use the old system's wording ("Cook Station", "Resource details", "Add to booking", "Additional Products", "Summary", "Continue").
