Two or three companies can be in at once as long as their equipment does not clash; "that's when we have a problem ... that's what **Avenue** is going to offer us."

### 1.3 How the client wants booking to work (recording 2, 0:00 to 4:30, summarised)
1. **"Ultimately it's all about booking the equipments."** The booking is "actually, really, just based on the equipment that they're booking" (recording 1, 1:06 to 1:12; recording 2, 3:14 to 3:34).
2. Example: there are two burners at a time. If one person selects the two burners, the other person cannot have them at the same time. The client confirmed ("Correct ... it really wouldn't boil down to a station; it will boil down to the equipment").
3. **Every company gets two prep tables.** "A booking without any equipment" still gives **at minimum two prep tables**. (Matches the sheet: "Minimum prep tables per company: 2".)
4. Some chefs come in and **need no cooking equipment**. Flavors wants Avenue to show that **all the cooking equipment is free** so it can let **one or two more companies** in at the same time ("maybe that company may only need prep tables").
5. Cook stations disappear; the sheet's "Spaces" tab is where the physical counts live.

### 1.5 Plans and hours (recording 2, 8:10 to 9:20)
- **10 hours per month** is the current minimum tier; it is a plan allowance. If a member **exceeds** it, the excess "will be counted in the **coming month's bill**" (matches D5, no charge at booking).
