Overview

What Phase 1 is

Phase 1 is an interim operating system for a shared-kitchen facility, built entirely inside Microsoft 365 — SharePoint, Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI. It gives members and staff a single, branded place to book the kitchen, stay compliant, check in and out, see payments and temperatures, and communicate — usable in a browser on desktop and mobile with no app install.

It is a single-site pilot: one facility, configured for the facility, designed to validate the workflows with real users. The full multi-client Avenue platform is Phase 2.

Modules

Core modules

The six core modules requested in the RFQ. Each is described with what it does, who uses it, and how it is built inside Microsoft 365.

1
Kitchen Bookings
Members request a station, table or equipment for a date and time. A shared calendar shows who has booked which station and equipment, and when — so members can plan around a busy kitchen. Staff approve requests and manage conflicts, and can book on a member’s behalf.
Request a bookingPick resource, equipment add-ons, date and time; the app checks for overlaps before submitting.
Power Apps
Shared calendarAll members see member name, station, equipment and time; private data stays private.
SharePoint list
Staff approval & on-behalfStaff approve/deny, resolve conflicts, and create bookings for a member with an audit stamp.
Power Automate
2
Check-In / Check-Out & Booking Alerts
Every booking requires an entry and exit checklist (wellness and food-safety items). Timed reminders go out before the booking; missed checklists are flagged to the member and escalated to staff. This is the timed-alert and escalation engine.
Entry & exit checklistsFixed question set, submitted in the app and logged with the member’s name and time.
Power Apps
Timed reminders (3/2/1 hr)Scheduled flows remind the member before start; each fires once, no duplicates.
Power Automate
Escalation & staff outreachOverdue checklists appear in a staff view with contact details and a logged one-off reminder.
Power Automate
3
Compliance Documents
Members (or staff on their behalf) upload permits, licenses, insurance and certifications. The system tracks expiration and flags each as current, expiring soon or expired, with generic reminder nudges to renew.
Document uploadStore documents with type, issue and expiry metadata; staff can upload on behalf of a member.
SharePoint library
Expiry tracking & flagsA daily flow sets Current / Expiring / Expired status and nudges before anything lapses.
Power Automate
4
Payments Dashboard
A read-only view of invoices, payments and balances synced automatically from QuickBooks Online. Members see only their own balance; staff see all. Payment is made in QuickBooks as today — this is visibility, not a payment gateway.
QuickBooks syncScheduled pull of invoices and balances, matched to each member; read-only.
Power Automate
Member-scoped balancesEach member sees their own invoices and status; staff get oversight of all accounts.
SharePoint list
5
Environmental Monitoring
A read-only display of refrigerator and freezer temperatures pulled from the existing Swift Sensors system, with a historical log for compliance recordkeeping. Swift Sensors keeps ownership of real-time breach alerting.
Live temperature tilesLatest reading per unit with Normal / Out-of-range status.
Power Automate (HTTP)
Historical logReadings stored over time in SharePoint for compliance records and reporting.
SharePoint list
6
Communication
Announcements, notices and newsletters posted centrally by staff. Members receive a generic email nudge to log in — no operational content is ever placed in the email body.
Announcements & noticesStaff publish to all members or by audience; content lives in the app.
SharePoint News
Generic email nudgeOn publish, a flow emails members a content-free “log in to view” prompt.
Power Automate

Added modules

Three additions confirmed after the demo, included in the Phase 1 scope.

7
Member Onboarding ADDED
A new member enters their details, selects a membership, and uploads their initial permits and documents. Once staff approve, the member gains full access to the rest of Avenue. Desktop/web only, as recommended — not a mobile function.
Self-service applicationProspective member submits business info, membership choice and initial documents.
External form
Staff approval & access grantStaff review and approve; the member is provisioned and gains platform access.
Power Automate
8
Reporting & Analytics ADDED
Dashboards for document-compliance status, monthly hours booked (per member and across the whole member base), and equipment usage to inform maintenance scheduling and life-expectancy planning.
Compliance & usage dashboardsAt-a-glance status across members, bookings and equipment.
Power BI
Hours & equipment reportingMonthly booked hours per member and member-wide; equipment usage trends.
Power BI
9
Operator Self-Service (Admin) ADDED
The site operator gets an admin tier: up to two admin accounts who can add and edit users, update membership plans, and post announcements for their facility. Doubleklick / the Client retains the super-admin role for major changes.
User & plan managementOperator admins add/edit members and adjust membership plans for their site.
Power Apps
Role tiersMember, operator-admin and super-admin, each scoped to the right level of control.

Access model

Who sees and does what

AreaHow it works
Member loginMembers sign in with their own personal email as external/guest users. Email authenticates identity only.
Shared booking visibilityAll members see the full booking calendar — name, station, equipment, date and time — to plan around a busy kitchen.
What stays privateInvoices and balances, compliance documents, and checklist answers are visible only to the member and staff.
Staff / operator adminManage bookings, approvals, users and plans across all member records; act on a member’s behalf with an audit trail.
Super-adminRetained by Doubleklick / the Client for major configuration and changes.
MobileThe Power Apps app runs on phones via browser or Microsoft’s free Power Apps app — bookings, payments, communication, sensors and check-in/out. Onboarding stays on desktop.
Delivery

Eight-week sequence

Delivered over approximately eight weeks. The core members use first is delivered early so onboarding and training can begin; the alert engine, reporting and onboarding complete toward the end.

WeekDeliverables
Week 1Site provisioning & Avenue branding, service account and security model, all SharePoint lists and libraries, Power Apps app shell with role detection.
Week 2–3Kitchen Bookings (calendar, request, approval, on-behalf) and Communication. Core testable from here.
Week 4Compliance Documents and Payments Dashboard (QuickBooks sync).
Week 5–6Environmental Monitoring (Swift Sensors) and the Check-In/Out & alert engine.
Week 7–8Member Onboarding, Reporting & Analytics, operator self-service; mobile pass, testing, UAT and handover.
Non-goals

Not in Phase 1

Deliberately out of scope for this interim pilot, to keep it focused.

#Not in this build
1No database-level double-booking lock — the app flags overlaps and staff approve.
2No payment gateway — payments stay in QuickBooks; the dashboard is read-only.
3No custom app-store app — mobile via browser or the Power Apps app; a branded native app is Phase 2.
4No multi-tenant / multi-client architecture — a single-site build for the Client [City].
5No duplicate real-time temperature alerting — Swift Sensors remains the system of record.
